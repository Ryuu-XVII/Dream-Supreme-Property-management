# Dream Supreme - Agent & Staff Manual

Welcome to the Dream Supreme Property Management Platform! This guide explains how to use the system day-to-day to manage your properties, deals, and commissions.

## 1. Managing the Deal Pipeline

The core of the platform is the Kanban-style Deal Pipeline. It tracks a transaction from the initial listing all the way to registration.

- **Creating a Deal**: Click "New Deal" from the Pipeline view. You'll need to select a Property (and an active Mandate if applicable).
- **Moving Stages**: Simply drag and drop a deal card from one column (e.g., `OTP Signed`) to the next (e.g., `Bond Approved`).
- **Progress Notes**: When moving a deal to a new stage, you will be prompted to add a Progress Note. This keeps the principal and your team informed of the status.

## 2. Compliance (FICA & FFC)

Our system strictly enforces property compliance rules.

- **Uploading FICA**: Inside any Deal, navigate to the "Documents" tab. You must upload IDs and Proof of Residence for all Buyers and Sellers before the deal can advance to Registration.
- **FFC Certificates**: Agents must keep their Fidelity Fund Certificates (FFC) up to date. You can upload your latest certificate in your **Profile Settings**. The system will automatically notify you 30 days before it expires.

## 3. Commission Tracking & Earnings

You have full transparency over your expected earnings.

- **Earnings Dashboard**: Navigate to the "Commission" tab to view your Year-to-Date (YTD) earnings, pending pipeline value, and completed payouts.
- **Deductions**: When a deal registers, the system automatically calculates your split based on your agency's rules. It will deduct Franchise Fees, Desk Fees, and any approved Marketing deductions before finalizing your net payout.
- **Reconciliation**: You can download a PDF reconciliation statement for any registered deal.

## 4. Notifications

You can control how the system alerts you to changes in your deals.
- Go to **Settings > Preferences** to choose whether you want immediate Realtime Emails, a Daily Digest, or just In-App notifications.
