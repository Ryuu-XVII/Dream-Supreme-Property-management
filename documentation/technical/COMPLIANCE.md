# Dream Supreme Property Management - Legal & Compliance Framework

This document outlines the regulatory frameworks the platform must adhere to. Non-compliance exposes the platform and the agencies using it to severe legal and financial penalties.

## 1. FICA (Financial Intelligence Centre Act) - AML & KYC

Real estate agencies are accountable institutions under FICA and must perform strict KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) checks.

- **Identity Verification:** The system must collect and securely store proof of identity (ID Document/Passport) for all buyers, sellers, landlords, and tenants.
- **Address Verification:** The system must collect and securely store proof of residence (e.g., utility bill not older than 3 months).
- **Status Tracking:** The system must provide a clear status for FICA compliance (e.g., `Pending`, `Verified`, `Rejected`) and prevent deals from progressing to financial stages if FICA is incomplete.
- **Auditability:** Every FICA approval/rejection must be logged with the timestamp and the identity of the compliance officer who reviewed it.

## 2. POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)

POPIA governs how personal data is collected, processed, and stored in South Africa.

- **Consent:** The system must ensure explicit consent is captured when collecting personal data.
- **Purpose Specification:** Data can only be collected for specific, explicitly defined purposes (e.g., facilitating a property transaction or FICA).
- **Security Safeguards:** Implement strict RLS and encryption (refer to `SECURITY.md`) to prevent data breaches.
- **Data Subject Participation:** The system must provide mechanisms for users to request access to their data, or request deletion (subject to FICA retention laws).

## 3. Data Retention Policies

FICA and POPIA have competing requirements regarding data retention:

- **POPIA** requires data to be destroyed when no longer needed for its original purpose.
- **FICA** legally requires accountable institutions to retain client records (identity, transaction details, correspondence) for **5 years** from the date the business relationship was terminated or the transaction concluded.
- **System Rule:** The platform must enforce a strict retention policy where transactional and identity data cannot be hard-deleted until the 5-year FICA retention period has elapsed. A soft-delete mechanism (e.g., `archived_at`) should be utilized to satisfy POPIA's visibility requirements without violating FICA.

## 4. ECTA (Electronic Communications and Transactions Act)

ECTA governs the legal validity of electronic signatures.

- **General Contracts (e.g., Leases, Mandates):** Standard electronic signatures (e.g., drawn signatures, click-to-sign) are legally binding.
- **Alienation of Land Act (Deeds of Sale / OTP):** In South Africa, the sale of immovable property legally requires an **Advanced Electronic Signature (AES)** (accredited by the SAPO) or a traditional wet-ink signature.
- **System Rule:** The platform's native e-signature tool can be used for mandates and leases. However, for an Offer to Purchase (OTP), the system must clearly indicate that a certified AES or wet-ink upload is required.
- **Cryptographic Hashing:** All signed documents generated by the system must be cryptographically hashed (SHA-256) upon completion. The hash must be displayed to prove the document has not been altered post-signature.
